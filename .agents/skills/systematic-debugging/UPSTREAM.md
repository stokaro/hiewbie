This is a focused adaptation of the reproduction, root-cause investigation,
hypothesis, and fix/verification workflow. Triggers are narrowed to unexplained
or persistent defects. Automatic TDD/verification skill chains and mandatory
subagent processes are omitted; repository-specific tests and required gates
remain necessary. No other Superpowers modules or hooks are installed.
These adaptations are not an upstream Superpowers release.
